* **Empirical psychology of regret (Gilovich, Medvec, Davidai):**
  * **Single biggest lifetime regret:** 76% of participants cite a failure related to their **ideal self** (unfulfilled dreams/aspirations), compared to 24% for their **ought self** (duties/obligations).
  * **Overall lifetime regret composition:** 72% of total reported lifetime regrets involve ideal-self discrepancies; 28% involve ought-self discrepancies.
  * **Temporal reversal of regret:** Short-term regrets are predominantly **actions/commissions** (vivid, immediate, manageable); long-term lifetime regrets are overwhelmingly **inactions/omissions** (open-ended missed opportunities).

* **Hedonic treadmill & life satisfaction decay:**
  * **Career achievement satisfaction decay:** Major achievements (promotions, salary increases) yield transient boosts in subjective well-being before returning to a baseline set-point (Brickman et al., 1978).
  * **Extrinsic vs. intrinsic goal orientation:** Prioritizing extrinsic goals (wealth, status, prestige) correlates with lower vitality, higher anxiety, and steeper satisfaction decay compared to intrinsic goals (relationships, community, growth) (Kasser & Ryan, 1996, 2001).
  * **Midlife well-being nadir:** Life satisfaction follows a universal U-shaped trajectory across 145 countries, reaching its global minimum around ages 47–50 before recovering in later adulthood (Blanchflower & Oswald, 2008).

### Agent research notes

* **Socioemotional Selectivity Theory (SST - Laura Carstensen):** Human goal prioritization depends on perceived Future Time Perspective (FTP). When time horizon is perceived as expansive (youth), people prioritize information gathering and status; when perceived as bounded, motivation shifts to emotional fulfillment and deep interpersonal relationships.
* **Terror Management Theory (TMT - Greenberg, Pyszczynski, Solomon):** Reminders of mortality trigger defensive cultural worldview adherence, but when consciously integrated, mortality salience serves as a catalyst for intrinsic value orientation and authenticity.
* **Regret Minimization Framework (Jeff Bezos):** Practical decision model projecting one's perspective to age 80 to evaluate choices based on minimizing long-term omission regrets rather than short-term friction.
