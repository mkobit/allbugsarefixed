## Scratch

#### Idea
Analyzing the cost of playing games in modern arcades, specifically focusing on the credit system, conversion rates, and the high cost per game.

#### Key observations
- I observed a modern arcade using a card swipe system instead of coins.
- Games appeared insanely expensive.
- A shooter game cost roughly $2.50 per play.
- A single swipe for the Contra game cost 10.4 credits.
- Credit bundles had varying price points:
  - Entry level: $20.
  - High tier: $120.

#### Research questions
- **Pricing structure:** Determine the actual dollar value of a credit.
  - If a Contra game (10.4 credits) is comparable to the shooter game ($2.50), then 1 credit ≈ $0.24.
  - If 1 credit ≈ $0.24, then the $20 entry bundle might provide roughly 83 credits.
- **Bonus credits:**
  - Need to investigate how many "bonus" credits are included in the $120 high-tier bundle to lower the effective cost per game.
- **Comparison:**
  - Compare this implied cost (approx. $0.24/credit) with standard pricing at major chains like Dave & Buster's or Round 1.

* **Dave & Buster's credit pricing (2026 standardized nationwide pricing):**
  * New card activation fee: $3.00 (includes 15 bonus chips).
  * $10.00 pack: 40 chips (4.00 chips/$, effective cost **$0.2500 per chip**).
  * $20.00 pack: 100 chips (5.00 chips/$, effective cost **$0.2000 per chip**).
  * $25.00 pack: 125 chips (5.00 chips/$, effective cost **$0.2000 per chip**).
  * $29.00 pack (Supercharged $25): 157 chips (5.41 chips/$, effective cost **$0.1847 per chip**).
  * Costco gift card stacking (20% off gift cards) + Legend Tier loyalty bonus (15% bonus chips): reduces effective chip cost to **~$0.0928 per chip**.
  * Average game cost: 6 to 7 chips per play (effective cost **$1.20 to $1.40 per play** at standard $20 pack rate; **$0.56 to $0.65 per play** under max stack discounts).
  * Coin pushers and high-tier redemption games: 10 to 12 chips per play (effective cost **$2.00 to $2.40 per play** standard).

  * Consolidated Revenue: ~$2.10B to $2.21B annually.

* **Ticket redemption economics & prize COGS:**
  * Target Cost of Goods Sold (COGS): **12% to 15%** of gross redemption game revenue.
  * Game ticket payout percentage: **18% to 24%** (formula: `(Wholesale Prize Cost / Gross Game Revenue) x 100`).
  * Retail vs. Wholesale markup: **2.0x standard prize markup** in ticket value calculation.
  * Target perceived value markup: **~4.0x wholesale cost** (items priced in tickets such that guests perceive retail store equivalence).
  * Single ticket wholesale valuation formula: `Ticket Value = (Total Wholesale Prize Spend) / (Total Tickets Redeemed)`. Typical valuation falls between **$0.005 and $0.010 per ticket** (half a cent to one cent per ticket).

### Agent research notes

* **Pricing tier psychology & high vs low reload bonus math:**
  * Low-tier reloads ($10 to $20) penalize casual players with higher unit costs ($0.20–$0.25/credit or chip).
  * High-tier reloads ($100 to $140) provide a 35% to 54% discount per credit/chip compared to the base tier, driving higher upfront cash collection and increasing unredeemed card balance breakage.
  * Breakage economics: customer balances left unspent on card systems represent 100% gross margin revenue until redeemed or written off per state escheatment regulations.

* **Effective cost per game play calculation formula:**
  * Formula: `Effective Cost per Play = (Total Dollar Spend / Total Credits Received) x Credits per Play`.
  * Example (Dave & Buster's): Standard $20 tier: `($20 / 100 chips) x 7 chips/play = $1.40 per play`. Max discount stack ($120 spend equivalent yielding chips at $0.0928/chip): `$0.0928 x 7 chips/play = $0.65 per play`.

* **Redemption game loop & ROI dynamics:**
  * Inverse entertainment-to-payout rule: games with high pure entertainment value (e.g. rhythm games, simulators, pinball) maintain low or zero ticket payouts (0%–5% payout equivalent). Quick-play redemption pushers and wheel games compensate for low play duration (10–15 seconds per play) with high ticket payout percentages (20%–30%) to maintain player retention.
  * Machine payback ROI calculation: A $12,000 machine charging $1.40 per play with 91.9% gross margin yields ~$1.29 gross profit per play. Payback period requires ~9,300 plays (~186 days at 50 plays/day).
