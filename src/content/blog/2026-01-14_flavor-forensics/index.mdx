* **Brand History & Origin Dates:**
  * **1958:** Gold Medal Products Company introduces blue raspberry flavor in snow cone syrups and cotton candy mixes (earliest recorded commercial use).
  * **1969:** FDA permanently approves FD&C Blue No. 1 for use in food and ingested drugs.
  * **1970:** Otter Pops launched by Jel Sert featuring mascot **Louie-Bloo Raspberry**.
  * **Early 1970s:** ICEE Company introduces Blue Raspberry slushie to differentiate raspberry from Cherry and align with brand red/blue logo.
  * **1976:** FDA bans FD&C Red No. 2 following toxicology concerns (Russian studies on rat carcinogenesis), accelerating industry adoption of FD&C Blue No. 1 for raspberry products.
  * **1993:** Perfetti Van Melle introduces **White Mystery Airheads** based on fan suggestion.
  * **Late 1990s:** Gatorade launches **Frost** sub-line featuring **Riptide Rush** (indigo/purple drink with grape-berry-passionfruit flavor profile).

* **Botanical Data (*Rubus leucodermis*):**
  * **Common Names:** Whitebark raspberry, blackcap raspberry, western raspberry.
  * **Native Range:** Western North America (Alaska/British Columbia south to California, east to Montana, Utah, Nevada).
  * **Morphology:** Glaucous stems and canes (whitish waxy bloom over dark bark). Ripe fruit is dark purple to black.
  * **Pigment Chemistry:** Color derived from anthocyanins (cyanidin-3-glucoside, cyanidin-3-rutinoside, cyanidin-3-sophoroside), not synthetic triarylmethane dyes.
  * **Divergence:** Plant name and dark purple fruit are botanically unrelated to synthetic blue raspberry candy flavor or FD&C Blue No. 1 dye.

### Agent research notes

* **Food Science & Flavor Formulations:**
  * **Synthetic Raspberry Profile:** Blend of esters (isoamyl acetate, ethyl acetate, ethyl butyrate), ionones ($\alpha$/$\beta$-ionone), raspberry ketone, maltol/ethyl maltol, and citric acid.
  * **Color Redundancy Solved:** Red fruit flavor overload (cherry, strawberry, watermelon, red raspberry) led manufacturers to select electric blue to reduce visual confusion in vending and slushie machines.
  * **Gatorade Riptide Rush:** Formulated under the "Frost" sub-brand (lighter profile). Uses FD&C Red 40 and FD&C Blue 1 to create visual indigo/violet color; flavor profile combines grape, wild berry, and passion fruit notes.

* **Manufacturing Mechanics (Mystery Flavors):**
  * **Dum Dums (Spangler):** Continuous production line transition. Switching from Batch A (e.g., Cherry) to Batch B (e.g., Butterscotch) creates an overlapping run of mixed candy in the extruder/cutters. Rather than stopping lines for washdown or discarding product, transitional candy is wrapped in "Mystery Flavor" packaging.
  * **Airheads (Perfetti Van Melle):** Continuous taffy extrusion. When changing flavor batches (e.g., Watermelon to Green Apple), dye pumps are shut off while flavor pumps transition. The uncolored white taffy containing the transitional flavor blend is wrapped as "White Mystery".

* **Flavor Myths vs. Reality:**
  * **Pink Lemonade Reality:** Historical 19th-century receipts/cookbooks show pink lemonade was originally tinted intentionally using fruit juices (raspberry, strawberry, cherry, or watermelon juice). Circus tales of dirty tights and dropped candies were "jackpot" stories (circus tall tales) amplified by news reports and memoirs.
  * **Blue Raspberry Natural Fallacy:** Natural *Rubus leucodermis* produces dark purple-black berries with waxy white-blue stem coating; commercial blue raspberry flavor contains 0% *Rubus leucodermis* extract.
