### Facts & data

- The Hawk: Netflix, 10-episode comedy, premiered 2026-07-16. Created by Will Ferrell, written by Chris Henchy. Ferrell plays Lonnie "The Hawk" Hawkins, 2004's #1 golfer chasing a comeback. (Wikipedia, Golfmagic)
- PGA Tour Studios partnered on the show — licenses real tour events, real sponsors, real players (Rickie Fowler, Justin Thomas appear). (HR review)
- In-show brand mentions/integrations: Chili's, Red Lobster, Carrabba's, Outback, Buffalo Wild Wings, Popeye's, McDonald's, Walmart, Mike's Hard Lemonade; chocolate bars incl. Milky Way (script slogan, mentioned ≥2×); Callaway (equipment sponsor), Malbon (apparel). (HR, NPR, MediaPost)
- Real event tie-ins referenced in-show: Waste Management Phoenix Open, Korn Ferry Tour. (MediaPost, HR)
- Official marketing partnerships (out-of-show, in-character):
  - SKIMS — Ferrell as Hawkins; Cotton Rib Tank (Chalk), Men's Tube Crew Sock (Chalk); Kim Kardashian narration. (Ad Age, Complex)
  - Mike's Hard Lemonade — Luke Wilson's character Golden Fisk is a Mike's sponsor in-show; "Mike's x The Hawk" retail products + merch sweepstakes. (HR partnerships)
  - Genesis — Jimmy Tatro's character Lance Hawkins; spot features the Genesis GV80. (HR partnerships)
- Press/bus tour, Ferrell fully in character: press conference at World Golf Hall of Fame, Pinehurst NC, 2026-06-18; East Coast bus tour; crashed Travelers Championship Pro-Am 2026-06-24. (Netflix Tudum, Heavy)
